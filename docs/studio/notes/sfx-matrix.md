# Sound-effect event matrix

`data/audio/sfx-matrix.json` lists the original sound effects, one row per `audio::SoundId`. It is generated by `tools/audio/sfx_matrix.py` from two inputs:

* the source at the current commit;
* the game's own `SOUND/M0..M11`.

```
python tools/audio/sfx_matrix.py --game <folder holding SOUND>          # write
python tools/audio/sfx_matrix.py --game <folder holding SOUND> --check  # stale?
```

All 23 rows are decoded from the real containers. The tool reads them the way `MFileLoader` and `VocDecoder` do:

* VOC entries take effect slots in load order (m0, m7, m8, m9, m10);
* CMF entries go to music, not to effect slots.

Call sites come from `git grep` at HEAD; no line number is typed by hand.

## Fields per row

* **`slot`, `id`**: mixer slot and `SoundId`.
* **`call_sites`**: `file:line` plus the line's code.
* **`trigger`, `scene`, `role`, `priority`**: when the effect plays and where. `role` is loop or one-shot. `priority` is one of ambience, ui, alert or feedback.
* **`reachability`**: one of three values.
  * *visited*: a gameplay call site exists. This is static, not observed at runtime.
  * *unverified*: no call site found. `WEAPON_2` has this value.
  * *unused*: proven never played. No row has it.
* **`original`**:
  * container and entry index, plus the container's sha256;
  * format: 8-bit unsigned mono, the VOC rate, samples and duration;
  * sha256 of the VOC bytes and of the PCM;
  * VOC block types and repeat count;
  * peak, clipped samples, DC offset, and leading/trailing quiet;
  * what the engine makes of it: 44.1 kHz s16 stereo frames and bytes.
* **`candidates`**: replacement leads (see below).
* **`fallback`**: `original`.
* **`selector`**: the Original/Enhanced choice. It does not exist yet.
* **`asset_check`, `engine_trigger_check`, `audible_review`**: these stay `open`. The tool never closes them.

`candidate_new_events` lists things the game does that make no sound now: guest enters, guest leaves, pizza served, kitchen ambience. All are marked "needs new trigger", and none counts as coverage. Ovens and payment have no discrete event in the views (`families_not_backed_by_events`).

## What decoding showed

* **Loops built into the VOC files.** m0 (crowd) and m7 (traffic) carry VOC repeat blocks with count 65535 (endless), and the ring (m8[10]) carries count 2. The engine ignores repeat blocks and loops through the mixer instead, so behaviour is unchanged. Keep this in mind if a replacement has its own loop data.
* **Quiet originals.** Restaurant crowd 1 peaks at 12/128 (about -20 dBFS); flame, bazooka and siren peak at 9–12/128. The chopper is the loudest effect, at 121/128.
* **Silence at the edges.**
  * POLICE_SIREN starts with 2.3 s of near-silence.
  * The ring has about 0.45 s of near-silence at each end; that is part of its ring cadence.
  * The weapon sounds carry 0.3–0.7 s quiet tails.
* **Rates.** 11,111 Hz for most effects, 22,222 Hz for the chopper and WEAPON_2, and 5,524 Hz for the flame and the siren.
* **WEAPON_2 is not a copy of the chopper.** It has nearly the same length (1.627 s at 22,222 Hz), but the waveforms are uncorrelated (peak correlation 0.07).

### pack-v0, revision 2 (built by `<cloud-work>/sfx-work/build_pack.py`, numpy, byte-reproducible)

The pack carries `manifest.json` in the engine's schema (`docs/studio/notes/sfx-overrides.md`: format `pthd-sfx-overrides`, version 1). Each entry has:

* the kebab-case event and its slot;
* the file's sha256;
* `source_sha256`, the original VOC bytes (the matrix's `voc_sha256`);
* gain, trims and loop start of 0, because all of that is baked into the files;
* `loop`, `fallback`, `origin`, `provenance` and `license`.

The loader limits shaped the loops. They are 120 s and 16 MiB per file and 64 MiB decoded for the whole pack, and one full crowd recording is 106 s and 18.7 MB. So each ambience loop is a window of its recording, picked for an even level and a small level step at the seam. Its tail is crossfaded into its head over 0.5 s equal-power, and the loop is then set to the original's loudness. The pack decodes to 39.0 MiB of the 64.

| Event | Window | Loudness | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| RESTAURANT_NOISE_1 | GuestRoom_low 34.5–83.0 s | -33.1 LUFS (original -33.1) | -8.2 dBFS |
| RESTAURANT_NOISE_2 | GuestRoom_mid 14.5–63.0 s | -28.7 LUFS (original -28.7) | -6.4 dBFS |
| RESTAURANT_NOISE_3 | GuestRoom_high 10.0–58.5 s | -19.5 LUFS (original -18.8; held 0.7 dB for a -1 dBFS peak) | -1.0 dBFS |
| TRAFFIC_1 (close zoom) | Urban_Traffic_Day 34.5–75.0 s | -26.4 LUFS (original -26.4) | -8.0 dBFS |
| TRAFFIC_2 (medium zoom) | Urban_Traffic_Night 45.5–86.0 s | -26.7 LUFS (original -26.7) | -9.0 dBFS |

* **DIAL_TONE_0..9**: authored (synthesized standard signal).
  * ITU-T Q.23 pairs, 120 ms, 5 ms edges, high group +2 dB.
  * RMS -11.7 dBFS, against the originals' -11.5.
* **PHONE_RINGING**: authored (synthesized standard signal). North American ringback, 440 + 480 Hz, 2 s on / 4 s off, in a 6 s loop that starts with the tone.
* **PHONE_PICKUP**: no entry, so the original plays. It is a recorded handset clatter (0.38 s, several transients); a two-transient synthetic click is thinner by design. The click is in `variants/` for the audible review and is not attached.
* **CHOPPER**: the restored PC3 cut as a 5-hit sequence at 0/130/260/390/520 ms.
  * Peak -3 dBFS; the loudest 600 ms is within 0.6 dB of the original's.
  * Loop start 0, so the m9 skip does not apply.

The sirens stay out: the PC3 clips are chirps, not a continuous loop.

### Checking a pack with the real loader

```
PLEGACY_TEST_GAME_PATH=<folder with PT.EXE> PLEGACY_SFX_PACK=<pack folder> test_plegacy "[sfx-pack]"
```

`tests/EffectPackCurationTests.cpp` (hidden) does three things:

* hashes the originals the way AudioManager does;
* calls `audio::load_effect_pack`;
* prints `replaced` or the loader's reason for every entry.

## Variation pools and layers (proposal for manifest v2)

None of these is attached to anything. Every pool below that is not the bed of an existing event "needs new trigger", and all review columns are open.

* The candidates are in `<cloud-work>/sfx-candidates/pools-v0/`. `pools.json` lists, per file:
  * the staged source and its sha256;
  * the decoded master and its sha256;
  * provenance and licence;
  * trim, fade and gain;
  * measured peak and loudness.
* Sources are bounded single-file copies: Kenney CC0 (Impact Sounds and RPG Audio, with each `License.txt`), PC3 loose files, and PC3 clips (byte ranges of the hash-checked `resources.resource`). Their receipt is in `sources-20261114/`.
* `build_pools.py` (numpy edits, ffmpeg decoding and measurement) rebuilds the pools byte for byte.

One-shots are trimmed to 2 ms before the onset (-40 dB from the peak) and 20 ms after the tail (-50 dB), with a 10 ms fade-out. They are levelled on active RMS (the frames within 30 dB of the peak), because ebur128 gating does not work on sounds this short. Peaks are capped at -3 dBFS.

### Layer plan per scene

Pools pick their variants with a cosmetic random state of their own, never the simulation RNG. The same variant never plays twice in a row, each pool has a voice limit of 1 (chatter 2), each play gets ±1.5 dB of random gain, and there is no pitch shifting unless the engine offers it. Pools duck by 6 dB while a phone or alert cue plays.

**Restaurant, dining room** (open, not the kitchen view, jukebox off; the same conditions as the crowd today):

* Bed: `restaurant-noise-1/2/3` by seating utilization (the existing events).
* Pool gain follows the level: -6 dB at level 1, -3 dB at level 2, 0 dB at level 3.

| Pool | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| cutlery | every 4–10 s | every 2–6 s | every 1–4 s |
| plates | 8–20 s | 5–12 s | 3–8 s |
| glasses | 10–25 s | 6–15 s | 4–10 s |
| chatter | off | 10–25 s | 6–15 s |

Event-bound one-shots, each needing a new trigger:

| payment | guest leaves |
| plates | pizza served (`waiter_delivered`), one variant |

At least 1.5 s apart per pool.

**Restaurant, kitchen view** (the crowd is off):

* Bed: `kitchen-busy` while open with utilization > 21 %, otherwise `kitchen-idle`.
* Pools: oven-door every 20–45 s (latch, then a door impact 150–300 ms later); pots every 8–20 s.

**City view:**

* Beds: `traffic-1` (close) and `traffic-2` (medium), as today.

**North Pole village** (once the village emits events). It is staged only, in `<cloud-work>/sfx-candidates/north-pole/candidates.json`.

* Bed: an outdoor night bed (PC3 Generic_Ambience_Night or Coastal_Night). It is unheard, so it is not known to be wind.
* Second bed: distant workshop at -40 LUFS (PC3 Factory_Idle_activity or construction_ambience).
* Pools: workshop taps every 6–15 s (Kenney impactWood_light / impactPlank_medium); bells rarely, every 30–90 s (PC3 Church_Idle_activity excerpts; Kenney impactBell_heavy only as a softened layer).
* Gaps:
